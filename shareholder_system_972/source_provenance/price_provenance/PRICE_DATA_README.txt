Adjustment: raw OHLC multiplied by cumulative Sina hfq factor effective on price date (latest factor date <= trading date). Factor data reused byte-for-byte from v1 same-day retrieval, source https://finance.sina.com.cn/realstock/company/{symbol}/hfq.js. Raw OHLC, multiplier and volume included in every CSV. Volume is unadjusted. Factors are current archive, not historical vintage. Sina hfq is corporate-action adjusted, not independently verified precise dividend reinvestment total return.
Indexes: index_000300=CSI300, index_000001=Shanghai, index_000905=CSI500, index_000852=CSI1000. These are unadjusted price indexes and not exact total-return benchmarks for adjusted stocks.

QA: price_qa.json verifies unique dates, positive finite prices, OHLC ranges, factor arithmetic, exact v1 overlap raw OHLC/factors, missing benchmark sessions and >40% adjusted close jumps. factor_transition_qa.json contains all effective factor transitions and raw/adjusted returns.
